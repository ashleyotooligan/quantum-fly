# Reproducible local results

Generated by `npm run experiments` from version 0.1.0. Thirty simulated runs, 640 ticks each. Seeds: 11, 17, 23, 29, 31, 41, 53, 67, 79, 97.

| Policy | Mean end distance (world units) | Mean odor | Runs with a source visit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| coherent | 368.98 | 0.100 | 0/10 |
| dephased | 359.42 | 0.137 | 0/10 |
| classical | 159.04 | 0.641 | 10/10 |

The classical reference is deliberately simple but effective in this world. This batch compares three declared policies, not a quantum advantage experiment. Both the circuit and the sampler execute on a classical CPU. No learned weights, quantum processor, biological recordings, or benchmark against a full fly connectome are involved.

Each matched seed uses one identical uniform number per tick, the same body initialization, the same network wiring, and the same world. Policies change paths, so subsequent sensors and neural states diverge. Shared seed is not identical sensory experience. The ten seeds are illustrative; no significance or generalization claim is made.

The phase/dephasing sweeps freeze the encoder parameters separately; they isolate a circuit effect without the confound of changing trajectories. At full dephasing, the final probabilities are independent of phi.
